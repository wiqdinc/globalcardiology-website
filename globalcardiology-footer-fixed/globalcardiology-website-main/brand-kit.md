# Global Cardiology Brand Kit

## Brand Identity
**Business Name:** Global Cardiology  
**Tagline:** "Caring for every heart, everywhere"

## Color Palette
- **Primary Blue:** #1e5ba8 (Trust, Medical Excellence)
- **Accent Blue:** #2876c9 (Professional, Reliable)
- **Light Blue:** #e8f4ff (Clean, Calming)
- **Teal Accent:** #2c9dad (Modern Healthcare)
- **Dark Text:** #1a1a1a
- **Medium Gray:** #666666
- **Light Gray:** #f5f5f5
- **White:** #ffffff

## Typography
- **Headings:** 'Poppins', sans-serif (Bold, Clean, Modern)
- **Body Text:** 'Inter', sans-serif (Professional, Readable)
- **Weight Guidelines:**
  - H1: 700 (Bold)
  - H2-H3: 600 (Semi-Bold)
  - Body: 400 (Regular)
  - Strong: 600 (Semi-Bold)

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Professional yet approachable** - Medical expertise with human compassion
- **Clear and reassuring** - Easy to understand medical information
- **Patient-focused** - Emphasizing care, comfort, and personalized treatment
- **Confident** - Backed by advanced technology and experienced professionals

## Key Messaging
- Comprehensive cardiac care services
- Advanced diagnostic technology
- Patient-centered approach
- Experienced and compassionate team
- Serving the Ottawa community with excellence

## Visual Style
- Clean, modern layouts
- Professional medical imagery
- Ample white space for clarity
- Smooth transitions and subtle animations
- Mobile-responsive design
- Accessible and easy to navigate
